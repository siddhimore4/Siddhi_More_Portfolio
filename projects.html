Bot Detection using Mouse Dynamics

Developed a behavior-based security framework that distinguishes human users from suspicious bot activity by analyzing mouse movement patterns. Using the Balabit mouse dynamics dataset, the system extracts behavioral features such as velocity, acceleration, jerk, and movement angle, along with statistical metrics. A K-Means clustering model establishes a baseline of typical human behavior, while Mahalanobis distance is used to identify anomalous activity against a 95th-percentile threshold. Suspicious users are then subjected to a second layer of verification through a draggable slider, allowing genuine users to proceed while blocking potential bots.

Matrubhoomi

Matrubhoomi is a civic data management platform developed to streamline the tracking and management of community issues. The platform focuses on structured issue reporting, data validation, and data-cleaning workflows to maintain reliable and organized information. It also provides analytical dashboards that transform collected civic data into meaningful insights, helping stakeholders understand issues, identify patterns, and make more informed, data-driven decisions.
